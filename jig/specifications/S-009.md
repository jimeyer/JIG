# Render Edges Distinguished by Type

The visualizer MUST render edges with visual distinctions based on their type.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Contains edges (module→class, class→function) render as thick solid lines
- Implements edges (code→spec) render as dashed lines with arrows
- Imports edges (module→module) render as thin lines with arrows
- Edge direction is visually clear through arrow markers
- Edge labels are readable when zoomed in

**Rationale**: Different edge types represent fundamentally different relationships. Visual distinction prevents confusion and aids in tracing specific relationship types.
