# Support Zoom and Pan Navigation

The visualizer MUST support zoom and pan navigation for exploring large graphs.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Mouse wheel zooms in/out on the graph
- Click-and-drag pans the graph viewport
- "Zoom to Fit" button fits entire graph in viewport
- "Zoom to Selection" button fits selected nodes in viewport
- Zoom level is smooth (not jumpy)
- Pan is responsive (no lag)
- Zoom and pan state persists when filtering or searching

**Rationale**: Graphs with 100+ nodes don't fit in a single viewport. Zoom and pan are essential for exploring different areas and focusing on specific regions.
