Let’s build the SELA Alignment Lexicon around that concept — a full family of words, metaphors, and states that describe how alignment behaves inside the V-I-B-T-C graph and inside an organization.

⸻

🧭 SELA Alignment Lexicon v0.1

1. Core Concept

Alignment is the degree to which all elements of the system — Values, Intents, Behaviors, Tests, and Code — are pulling in the same direction, telling the same story, and delivering on the same promise.

Alignment is both a technical condition and a cultural condition.
It describes the health of the product, the clarity of intent, and the unity between strategy, design, and implementation.

⸻

2. Anchor Terms

Term	Description	Common Use
Align	To bring different elements into agreement or a shared orientation.	“Let’s align our tests with the new behavior.”
Alignment	The state of being in agreement or harmony; coherence of purpose and execution.	“This release brought business and engineering back into alignment.”
Misalignment	A gap or inconsistency between intent and realization.	“We saw misalignment between our product story and what the app actually does.”
Realign	To re-establish shared understanding after change.	“After shifting the value statement, SELA triggered a realignment.”
Auto-align	When the SELA system automatically harmonizes related nodes after a small change.	“Behavior updates auto-aligned with the corresponding tests.”
Alignment Pass	A review or system operation that checks and restores alignment across the graph.	“We run an alignment pass before every milestone.”
Alignment Debt	Accumulated misalignment not yet reconciled (similar to technical debt).	“Skipping the intent review created some alignment debt we’ll need to pay off.”
Alignment Loop	The recurring cycle of detect → realign → verify.	“The SELA alignment loop runs after every merge.”


⸻

3. Related Verbs (the “alignment verbs”)

Verb	Meaning in SELA context	Human-friendly metaphor
Align	Bring elements into agreement.	“Align code to behavior.”
Realign	Adjust after change to restore agreement.	“Realign intent with new business value.”
Reconcile	Settle differences between two interpretations.	“Reconcile the old tests with the updated feature.”
Translate	Carry meaning across boundaries.	“Translate business intent into engineering behavior.”
Integrate	Merge aligned fragments into a coherent whole.	“Integrate new component into the aligned graph.”
Clarify	Make intent or behavior more explicit.	“Clarify why this test exists.”
Surface	Expose hidden misalignment.	“SELA surfaced drift between two subsystems.”


⸻

4. States of Alignment

State	Description	Example Language
Aligned	Everything coheres — shared understanding and consistent behavior.	“VIBTC graph fully aligned.”
Mostly Aligned	Minor differences; acceptable within tolerance.	“98% aligned — small test lag.”
Misaligned	Intent and behavior diverge noticeably.	“Feature works but contradicts product story.”
Unaligned	Nodes exist without any traceable connection.	“Legacy code unaligned with any current intent.”
Drifting	Alignment degrading over time.	“Drifting alignment since last update.”
Pending Alignment	Known change awaiting reconciliation.	“Intent updated, code pending alignment.”


⸻

5. Metrics & Signals

Metric	Description	Interpretation
Alignment Index	Quantitative score (0-1) of graph coherence.	“Current alignment index: 0.93.”
Drift Rate	Rate of misalignment growth over time.	“Drift rate up after the API refactor.”
Alignment Debt	Weighted count of unresolved tensions.	“Alignment debt decreasing as tests update.”
Confidence	Certainty that current links are valid.	“High confidence in test-behavior alignment.”
Coverage Map	Visualization of which Values/Intents have downstream alignment.	“All Intents mapped; two Behaviors unaligned.”

6. SELA System Roles (metaphorically)

Role	Description
The Aligner	Core SELA engine that detects misalignment and proposes realignments.
The Facilitator	Human (PM, tech lead) who decides which alignment proposals to accept.
The Alignment Graph	The VIBTC structure itself — a living network of commitments.
The Alignment Session	Human-in-loop interaction to review and confirm realignments.
Alignment Policy	Rules for automatic vs. manual alignment (how far the system can act).

7. Everyday Speech Patterns

Situation	Natural phrasing SELA could use
Healthy system	“Everything’s aligned — strategy, code, and customer value are moving together.”
After a change	“We’ve shifted our intent; SELA’s working on a realignment.”
When drift detected	“Looks like the implementation has drifted — let’s get it back in alignment.”
Conflict between teams	“Marketing changed the story but the product hasn’t caught up — misalignment across Value and Behavior.”
New feature introduction	“Add the new Value and let SELA propagate alignments downstream.”
End of cycle	“Alignment report: 94% coherence, 3 pending re-alignments.”

8. Organizational Resonance
	•	Strategic alignment — leadership and product vision map cleanly to what teams are building.
	•	Operational alignment — cross-functional teams share rhythm and clarity.
	•	Technical alignment — software structure mirrors intended behavior.
	•	Cultural alignment — shared language and trust in what “done” means.

“SELA gives us technical alignment that mirrors organizational alignment — when the product story changes, the codebase knows.”

9. Emotional and cultural texture

Use tone that feels human, cooperative, and forward-moving:
	•	“We’re back in alignment.”
	•	“Let’s check alignment before we launch.”
	•	“There’s a small alignment gap after that pivot.”
	•	“Nice — full alignment across the stack.”

It reads like a team conversation, not a tool log.
